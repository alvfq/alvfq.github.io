## Course Overview

The course combines theory with examples from real clinical practice and interactive exercises. The course has a mid-term and final evaluation, in which participants will reflect and evaluate AI systems relevant for their own work or study area.

The course provides the fundamental understanding to better assess, interpret and analyze the possibilities and limitations of utilizing AI in healthcare (such as AI-enabled medical devices), and how one can take better decisions when appraising their need, implementing and using them.

The course is self-paced and all teaching is fully digital. Course content includes pre-recorded lectures, practical exercises and articles. All content is available from the first day, such that you can decide where and when to learn. Course content is split in short videos, such that it is easier for you to watch the lectures in your every day.This online course is intended for doctors, nurses, leaders and other professionals in the healthcare sector that want to have a better understanding on the development and appraisal of artificial intelligence (AI)-enabled medical devices from an applied perspective.

**Learning outcomes**:

1. Knows the difference between generative AI, machine learning, and deep learning.
2. Knows how to recognise and categorise systems based on their input, output and intended use.
3. Knows the concepts of safety, reliability, performance and utility applied to AI systems in healthcare.
4. Recognises key considerations when training and evaluating AI systems in healthcare related to data, model performance and AI model selection.
5. Is familiar with AI-enabled medical devices regulations, including EU AI Act, CE markings, and FDA.
6. Knows and understands the concepts of drifts, data quality, performance metrics, and internal/external evaluations.
7. Understands the concept of utility and the challenges associated with implementing and using AI-enabled medical deices

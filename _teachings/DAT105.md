An intuitive introduction to artificial intelligence, open to anyone who wants to understand what AI is and what it can and cannot do. No programming background is assumed.

The course is fully digital, built from recorded lectures and structured in modules that can be fitted around work or study commitments.

## Structure

Four independent blocks:

1. **What AI is** — the essential concepts and the common terminology of the field.
2. **Before you use AI** — what to consider when deciding whether AI suits the problem at hand.
3. **Generative AI** — large language models, ChatGPT, and recent breakthroughs.
4. **Ethics** — the implications of deploying AI, and how to reason about them.

The blocks are independent, so they can be taken in the order that suits you.

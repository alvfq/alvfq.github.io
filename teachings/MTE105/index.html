An online course for doctors, nurses, managers and other professionals working in the healthcare sector who want a better understanding of how AI-enabled medical devices are developed and appraised.

The emphasis is applied rather than theoretical. The question the course keeps returning to is not how a model works internally, but how a clinician or a manager should judge whether a given AI-enabled device is fit to be used on their patients — and what evidence that judgement should rest on.

Who it is for

Healthcare professionals without a technical background: clinicians, nurses, and those in leadership or procurement roles who are being asked to evaluate or adopt these systems.
